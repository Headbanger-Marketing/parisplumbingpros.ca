FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

Pricing, licensing, emergencies, and the water itself, covered below.

Are you licensed to work on Paris homes?

Yes, licensed and insured, with permits pulled through the city for any Paris job that requires one and inspections passed before completion.

Can you get here fast if the basement is flooding?

Yes. Floods, backups, and burst lines in Paris and the immediate Brant roads get priority scheduling, evenings included where needed.

Our tank is in a stone cellar with a low ceiling. Take the job?

Happily. Cramped access and awkward exits describe half the water heaters in Paris. The plan accounts for the space before anything moves.

Would you repipe a heritage house or patch the leaks?

When leaks repeat and pressure fades, patching has stopped paying. We repipe with minimal access cuts and keep the original finishes as untouched as the job allows.

Does living near the rivers change basement plumbing?

It does. High water and surcharging mains put low-street basements at risk, so properly sized sump systems and backwater valves are worth real money here.

Is Paris water hard?

Moderately, and the wells outside town run harder with iron sometimes along for the ride. A test tells us which, and the treatment follows the result.

What does a quote cost and how firm is it?

Quotes are free and the written figure is the figure. You approve it before work starts and nothing gets added at the end.

Do you travel beyond Paris itself?

Yes, St. George, Glen Morris, Mount Pleasant, Ayr, and Brantford sit inside the regular service radius.

Do you serve areas outside of Paris, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Paris and nearby communities including St. George, Glen Morris, Mount Pleasant, Ayr, and the surrounding area.
